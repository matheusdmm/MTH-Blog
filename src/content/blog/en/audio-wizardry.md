Today, I want to do a little experiment. I want to write about the stuff that I love to use when I'm writing new songs, that being in the digital realm (VST plugins) or the hardware domain (Pedals and instruments).

## Everything has a beginning

I never thought much about the proccess, this includes the things that I used to like (and some I still love) until very recently.

At first, I just want to play and get along with other musicians (this was around 2010-2014). I had VERY low budget for basically everything. The most expensive thing that I could have was the guitar and bass, and not by much. Played a bunch of Squiers and Tagimas and SX's from that era. Honest instruments, specially the Squiers and Tagimas (From classic vibe and special series respectively).

Pedals were always expensive and honestly, I did not got much into them until quite later. At this time, I was playing hardcore punk, a bit of indie and brit rock, so distorton was all that I need basically.

From Black flag, Ramones, Fugazi, Rancid, Kings of Leon, Oasis, Blur and some other bands, it pretty much started everything. The raw energy and the explosion was there. I got hooked.

A loud amplifier, the cord straight from the guitar and the jam was ready to be started. The tool during this point was a old Meteoro MGR-50, the sound was crappy, but at least was loud and the fun could be had.
